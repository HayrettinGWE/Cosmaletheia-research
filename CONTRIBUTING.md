# Contributions

CosmAletheia Research is currently a **curated research repository**.

External pull requests, code submissions, datasets, prompts and research text are **not currently accepted**. This avoids ambiguous ownership, provenance and licensing while the public research surface is being established.

Discussion, critique and research contact are welcome through appropriate GitHub channels, but sending material does not grant the project permission to use it and does not create a collaboration agreement.

A separate contribution and inbound-license policy may be published later.
