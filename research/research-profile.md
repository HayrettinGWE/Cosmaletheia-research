# Research Profile — CosmAletheia

## Origin
The project asks how many possibilities, under constraints, can form stable structures that carry information, identity and function — and how such structures can become the starting point of another level.

The biographical origin reaches back to Hayrettin Atas' youth. That is biography, not dated technical evidence. Modern terminology is not retroactively assigned to childhood ideas. A private source archive provides dated direct statements from 2023 onward; versioned technical artifacts begin in 2026.

## Intuition as hypothesis generator
A recurring candidate grammar is:

> possibility / variation → boundary and milieu → difference or pressure → feedback / selection → stable or metastable form → function → renewed scale transition

This is not a law of nature.

**Counter-rule:** Similarity is not evidence.

An intuition becomes research-relevant only when it can be made precise enough to compare, test, falsify or discard.

## Technical direction
CosmAletheia investigates systems where exploration is separated from binding and authorization; provenance survives relevant state transitions; stable rules are separated from uncertain experience; role boundaries remain explicit; consequential effects remain separately authorized; and internal state changes may create task candidates without creating new authority.

## Central question
How can an agentic AI system absorb, compare and develop experience without losing provenance, validity, role boundaries and human control over consequential effects?

Natural-science, mathematical and philosophical comparisons are hypothesis spaces, not evidence.
