# Provenance Boundary

CosmAletheia distinguishes:
1. **thought history** — dated statements documenting stages of a developing question,
2. **artifact history** — versioned architecture, policy, design or code,
3. **operational evidence** — evidence that a mechanism actually ran,
4. **experimental evidence** — controlled results,
5. **scientific validation** — a stronger claim not implied by the above.
