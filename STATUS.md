## Status vocabulary
- **Artifact-backed** — a corresponding technical artifact exists privately.
- **Design / partial implementation** — a specification or partial mechanism exists.
- **Research hypothesis** — requires experiment and may be rejected.
- **Not claimed** — explicit boundary against overstatement.

## Artifact-backed
Private technical history contains artifacts for Core/Corpus separation, provenance markers, regression/replay structures, candidate gates, separated roles and selected hash-linked audit tooling.

## Design / partial implementation
Controlled-autonomy / membrane architecture; validity and authorization boundaries; persistent identity versus changing state/fingerprint; structured hand-offs and receipts.

## Research hypotheses
Internal state changes may generate useful task candidates without granting execution authority; recurring transition structures may help across architectural scales; bounded variation may improve adaptation while preserving provenance and rollback; stability should be evaluated through reproducible observables.

## Explicitly not claimed
Artificial consciousness; validated new physics; scientific priority; universal fractality; numerological laws; zero-point energy as an AI mechanism; demonstrated autonomous self-development; or that existing code is a general effectiveness proof.

## Evidence rule
A historical statement documents a stage of thought, not automatically a later implementation. A commit documents an artifact at a time, not the origin date of an idea. Code demonstrates existence, not effectiveness.
