## Research hypotheses

Internal state changes may generate useful task candidates without granting execution authority; recurring transition structures may help across architectural scales; bounded variation may improve adaptation while preserving provenance and rollback; stability should be evaluated through reproducible observables.

## Explicitly not claimed

## Evidence rule

A historical statement documents a stage of thought, not automatically a later implementation. A Git artifact anchor does not by itself establish the original date of an idea or historical public availability. Code existence is distinct from execution and effectiveness. Repetitions and copies of the same source are not independent confirmations. Publication of a candidate is not its scientific validation.
