# Controlled Autonomy

Controlled autonomy means increasing useful exploration without silently increasing authority.

```text
explore → propose → verify/bind → authorize → effect
```

A model may explore broadly while remaining unable to promote its own hypothesis to canonical truth, expand its own capability boundary, reuse expired validity without re-checking, or create consequential external effects without required authorization.

The objective is not maximum autonomy. It is **auditable autonomy with explicit effect boundaries**.
