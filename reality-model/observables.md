# Observables

The project prefers measurable stability over statements that a result merely "looks stable".

Candidate observables include:
- repeatability across runs
- regression pass/failure trends
- provenance retention
- contradiction detection
- rollback success
- unauthorized-effect attempts
- candidate survival/decay
- formally defined graph or state-transition invariants

Simulation-oriented spectral or graph measures may be investigated, but metrics should be specified before interpreting results.

## Anti-tuning rule
Hypothesized mathematical signatures may only be evaluated with predefined post-hoc measurements. They must not be injected into the dynamics as parameters and then counted as discoveries.
