# Realitätsmodell

The Realitätsmodell is one research strand within CosmAletheia.

It investigates whether a compact grammar of variation, feedback, selection, stabilization and scale transition can provide useful architectural hypotheses for information and agent systems.

```text
variation
→ feedback / correlation / self-reference
→ selection
→ condensation / stabilization
→ coarse-graining / scale transition
→ renewed variation
```

This is **not presented as validated physics**.

Physical, biological and mathematical concepts may be comparison spaces, but they must be translated into explicit software mechanisms, observables or falsifiable experiments before carrying technical weight.

## From the model language to testable candidates

The [formal-candidate register](../research/formal-candidates.md) separates four conceptual relations from their possible software translations and source histories. The [test specifications](../experiments/formal-candidate-tests.md) propose comparisons and failure cases. These are research plans, not evidence that the complete loop has been implemented or that noise/reflection necessarily generates intelligence.

For FC-04, parallel branch development and later comparison are a proposed way to postpone selection. They do not establish a new physical meaning of phase or binding. See also [observables](observables.md) and [project status](../STATUS.md).
