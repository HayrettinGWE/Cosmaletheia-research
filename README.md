# CosmAletheia Research

**Independent technical research & engineering by Hayrettin Atas**

CosmAletheia is an experimental research program on provenance-bound multi-agent systems, controlled autonomy, persistent knowledge structures, self-organization, and reproducible evaluation.

## Why this repository exists

This repository is the **public research surface** of CosmAletheia. It is intentionally separate from private historical workspaces and source archives.

The public surface is designed to distinguish:

- documented architecture and private technical artifacts;
- design proposals;
- research hypotheses;
- operational or experimental evidence, when available.

The project grew from a longer line of questions about how information, identity, memory, boundaries, feedback and selection can form stable structures while remaining capable of change. A private source-archive review reports stages of that development from 2023 onward; checked technical artifact anchors begin in February 2026. This public repository began in October 2026. These are different histories.

## Current research focus

- **Core / Corpus separation** — stable rules and definitions separated from a larger, uncertain experience and evidence space.
- **Provenance** — relevant claims and state changes should remain traceable to sources, versions or artifacts.
- **Regression against drift** — repeated tests and replayable baselines as a proposed control surface, with tooling reported in the private artifact history.
- **Candidate gates** — generated or retrieved material does not automatically become canonical.
- **Controlled autonomy** — exploration, binding and authorization, and external effect are distinguished.
- **Role separation** — collection, curation, criticism/verification, implementation and audit as distinct functions.
- **Realitätsmodell** — variation, feedback, selection, stabilization and scale transition as hypotheses to operationalize and test, not established laws of nature.

## Central research question

> How can an agentic AI system absorb, compare and develop experience without losing provenance, validity, role boundaries and human control over consequential effects?

A second question follows:

> Under what conditions can internal state changes generate new, testable task candidates without silently granting the system new authority?

## Research discipline

**Possibility may be broad. Binding requires evidence and boundaries. Effect requires authorization.**

Analogies from biology, physics, mathematics or philosophy are hypothesis generators. Similarity is not evidence. A useful analogy must be made precise enough to test, compare or reject. Publication approval is not scientific validation or operational Core promotion.
