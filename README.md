# CosmAletheia Research

**Independent technical research & engineering by Hayrettin Atas**

CosmAletheia is an experimental research program on provenance-bound multi-agent systems, controlled autonomy, persistent knowledge structures, self-organization, and reproducible evaluation.

> **Status:** research prototype / independent research project.  
> **Not peer reviewed. No claim of artificial consciousness, validated new physics, or scientific priority.**

## Why this repository exists

This repository is the **public research surface** of CosmAletheia. It is intentionally separate from private historical workspaces and source archives.

The public surface is designed to make four things distinguishable:

1. what has been **implemented or represented as an artifact**,
2. what currently exists as **design**,
3. what remains a **research hypothesis**, and
4. what evidence would be needed to test or reject it.

The project grew from a longer line of questions about how information, identity, memory, boundaries, feedback and selection can form stable structures while remaining capable of change. A dated private source archive documents stages of that development from 2023 onward; the technical artifact history begins in 2026.

## Current research focus

- **Core / Corpus separation** — stable rules and definitions are separated from a larger, uncertain experience and evidence space.
- **Provenance** — relevant claims and state changes should remain traceable to sources, versions or artifacts.
- **Regression against drift** — repeated tests and replayable baselines are used as a control surface.
- **Candidate gates** — new material does not become canonical merely because it was generated or retrieved.
- **Controlled autonomy** — exploration, binding/authorization and external effect are separate layers.
- **Role separation** — collection, curation, criticism/verification, implementation and audit are treated as distinct functions.
- **Self-organization as a research model** — variation, feedback, selection, stabilization and scale transition are treated as hypotheses to operationalize and test, not as established laws of nature.

## Central research question

> How can an agentic AI system absorb, compare and develop experience without losing provenance, validity, role boundaries and human control over consequential effects?

A second question follows from this:

> Under what conditions can internal state changes generate new, testable task candidates without silently granting the system new authority?

## Research discipline

CosmAletheia deliberately allows a broad hypothesis space while keeping the binding and effect spaces narrower.

**Possibility may be broad. Binding requires evidence and boundaries. Effect requires authorization.**

Analogies from biology, physics, mathematics or philosophy are treated as hypothesis generators. Similarity is not evidence. A useful analogy must be made precise enough to test, compare or reject.

## Public / private boundary

This repository does **not** contain raw private chats, personal source archives, private anchor mappings, credentials, internal workstation paths, or the private Git history used for provenance reconstruction.

Historical provenance is summarized only to the level needed to make the development path auditable without publishing private source material.
